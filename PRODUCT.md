# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Individuals, families, and businesses in North Carolina and South Carolina who need insurance guidance and want a straightforward way to request options from a local independent advisor.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces Patrick Wilson Financial, explains the available insurance categories, and routes qualified visitors into a short quote-request funnel without adding friction or unnecessary pressure.

## Brand Personality

Premium, grounded, trustworthy. The voice should feel personal and advisor-led, with clear insurance language and no unsupported claims.

## Anti-references

Do not use generic lead-generation hype, invented reviews or credentials, aggressive sales copy, physical address claims, or unrelated business-growth language. Preserve the existing navy, gold, and bone identity.

## Design Principles

- Keep Patrick Wilson visible as the human advisor behind the service.
- Preserve the short quiz-funnel path and avoid adding public address fields.
- Use specific insurance language instead of broad consulting claims.
- Communicate local service clearly: Serving North Carolina & South Carolina.
- Build trust through believable facts, not invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for production-quality contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, readable responsive type, and reduced-motion respect for animation-heavy interactions.
